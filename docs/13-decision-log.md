## Performance and accessibility targets
LCP < 2.5s (h1 text), CLS < 0.1 (all media sized), INP < 200ms (no work in scroll handlers). Contrast: ink on paper 15.5:1; stone on paper 4.7:1; stone on the roost 5.4:1; band copy 5.2:1 to 17:1 (docs/03). Keyboard: skip link, rows are links, Invite is a button, visible focus everywhere, aria-live bird count, aria-label on split headings, no hover-only content on touch.
